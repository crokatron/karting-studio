Analysis settings & how scores work

The attached file contains precomputed S-shaped fits for seven drivers. Exact matching laps use those values. Other sessions use an iteratively fitted S-shaped curve. Recalculate all trends applies the settings above to every driver. These are estimates; an unusually slow lap does not prove a crash or pit stop.

Balanced component ratings for best and pace: 5 stars for the best, 1 for the worst, scaled between. Equal values receive 3. Pace is the mean clean-lap difference from the group median at the same lap number. Consistency is scatter around the trend; the group median gets 3 stars, with one star per scaled median absolute deviation. Zero spread receives 3. Incidents: 5 minus half a star per flagged lap, minimum 1. Balanced driving averages the four ratings, rounded to half a star. Peak-speed stars use only the best eligible lap: 5 for the fastest, 4.5 within 2%, 4 within 4%, then half a star less per further 2%, minimum 1. Equal times receive equal stars. Exact best-lap rank is shown separately; tied stars do not mean equal lap times. Insufficient data shows a dash.

Automatic rules: laps above the pit threshold are excluded from fitting. Laps over the incident threshold above the fitted trend are flagged. A final lap slower than the preceding three clean laps' average plus the tolerance is marked cool-down. Saved manual incident exclusions take priority. These model flags do not alter your saved source times or manual exclusions.

Lap-number comparisons assume broadly comparable starts. Different start times, kart performance or changing conditions can bias ratings. No sector or confirmed incident information was supplied.

FROM THE TIMING SHEETS

A real archive. Room to grow.

What’s included

The “Karting Lap Time Archive – All Drivers – 12 Oct 2026” workbook from Karting lap times list, plus two photographed Al Ain Raceway results sheets dated 7 March 2025. All participants are retained, with repeat-driver flags calculated from their session history.

Times are in seconds at millisecond precision. Pit entries and duplicate forwarded reports were excluded in the source archive. Email and mailbox links are not shared here.

Al Ain photos include kart numbers and best-lap classifications, but only the two fastest lap times per driver. Other sessions have full recorded lap lists. No sector times, confirmed race finishing positions or track configurations were supplied. The portal cannot infer those from lap times.
